# Visual checklist

Run this on the rendered canvas or preview, not on the source. For each defect
record where it is, what is wrong, what is expected, the evidence, and the fix.

## Meaning

- The visual explains the feature and the benefit without a caption.
- Rate the takeaway separately from how well the shell is drawn.
- Labels are understandable without their icons.
- Requested removals are gone. Every claim is supported by the product,
  including claims that arrived in the brief or were already live, judged
  sentence by sentence as a visitor reads it: a list followed by a place
  claims the place for the whole list.
- Product graphics are built from the real editor, shell, chat, panels and
  controls. Simplified to clarify, never to invent. A UI with no real capture
  is named in text, not drawn.
- Variants are compared against the user's job, not novelty.

## Identity

- Typography, spacing, dividers, cards, controls, radii and tokens match the
  references, checked section by section with the reference open beside the
  work.
- No pattern transplanted from another part of the product because it exists.
- Logos are verified, legible in each theme.
- Popovers sit next to their triggers. Arrows point at the right thing.
- Copy is skimmable and specific. No AI jargon, filler eyebrows, em dashes, or
  forced slang.

## Bounds

- Dimensions, alignment, aspect ratio and placement of every graphic.
- No accidental cropping or page overflow. Intentional overflow kept.
- Clipping is not "fixed" by disabling containment everywhere.
- Adjacent graphics share dimensions and framing.
- Step graphics: each a different, settled moment; a frame caught
  mid-transition, or reused from another step or the demo, is a defect, and
  disclosing the reuse does not clear it.
- The frame shows what its caption names. If it cannot, the caption changes.
- One project per page: every graphic shows the same palette, names and app.
- No stage chrome from another medium (email or video eyebrows, title bands,
  player chips) inside a product graphic.
- The phone demo is the desktop demo scaled, never a crop that loses the
  header or a section.
- The smallest label in every graphic is readable at the size shown; shrink
  is not a crop. A graphic that fills a column is the same width as its
  neighbours.
- At narrow widths, padding shrinks before content does.

## Readability and themes

- Text, arrows, buttons, borders, overlays on light and dark.
- Muted and disabled states, not only primary content.
- No accidental white rectangles in dark mode.
- Contrast and type size at real display size. Enlarged text where relevant.

## Responsive

- Sweep narrow to wide and back. Three screenshots are not a sweep.
- Both sides of every breakpoint. Record range, widths, failures.
- Long copy, wrapping, stacking, navigation, image bounds, popovers, touch
  targets.

## Interaction

- Hover, focus, expanded, selected, disabled, loading, empty, error, success.
- Keyboard and touch. Cancel and recover. Nothing important hidden behind a
  panel.

## Evidence

- Review the change, not the summary of the change.
- Label captures with version, viewport, theme and state.
- Separate observed from assumed, and your review from the user's approval.
